# Phase 8 — Polish

Status: outline only. Deliberately thin.

Goal: the things worth doing once the system works, and not before.

## Candidates

- R8.1 Privacy-respecting analytics. No third-party tracking scripts; the page
  weight budget and the no-JavaScript requirement both constrain this.
- R8.2 `/api/resume` as a documented public JSON endpoint, with the schema
  published alongside it.
- R8.3 Theming beyond dark and light, if there is a reason.
- R8.4 A fresh portrait.

## Constraints

- R8.5 Nothing here may violate the Phase 3 performance budget or the
  no-JavaScript-to-read requirement.

## Note

Per-role resume variants moved out of this phase into Phase 6, where they
belong. They were the highest-value item on this list and did not deserve to
sit in polish.

Anything that arrives here and turns out to matter should be promoted into a
real phase with its own requirements rather than accumulating as a wishlist.
